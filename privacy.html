DoTheDeed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

DoTheDeed is provided by Xiru An. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles information.

Your records

DoTheDeed stores your events and records in the app’s storage on your device. Records may include event names, timestamps, geographic coordinates, location accuracy, time-zone information, and place names. The app uses this information to display your records, maps, and activity statistics.

You do not need an account. The app does not send your record database to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell your records.

Location and Apple services

When you create a location-based record, the app requests access to your location while using the app. With permission, the map may also display your current position. The app does not continuously record your route in the background.

Maps and place-name lookup use Apple’s MapKit and geocoding services. These services may communicate with Apple to load map content and translate coordinates into place names. Unresolved locations may be retried when the app is opened or when you request a retry. Apple handles data associated with its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

You can change location access in iOS Settings. Without location access, you can view existing records, but creating a new location-based record is unavailable. The app currently does not offer manual location entry.

Backups and sharing

You may choose to export a JSON backup through the system file interface. A backup contains your events and detailed records, including locations and timestamps. Exported backups are not password-encrypted by DoTheDeed. Keep them somewhere you trust.

You may preview and share a stats card using the system share interface. Cards omit recorded place names, coordinates, and dates. You choose whether to include your event name and area count. Services or people you choose to receive exported data handle it independently of DoTheDeed.

The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Depending on your settings, Apple’s device backup and restore services may include app data.

External links

The app may open a support, privacy, or social profile page in an in-app browser. Those sites receive the ordinary information sent when visiting a web page and operate under their own privacy terms. Opening an external page does not upload your DoTheDeed record database.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in app storage until you delete them or remove the app and its local data. Archiving an event retains its records; it is not deletion. To remove an individual record, swipe left or long-press it in the record list. To access an archived event’s records, restore the event first.

If a stored file cannot be read, the app may retain a recovery copy so it can be exported. Deleting the app removes its local storage, while offloading may retain it. Deleting local data does not delete backups you previously exported, system backups, or copies shared with other people. The developer cannot remotely access, restore, or delete your on-device records.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address and whatever information you choose to send. It is used to respond and investigate issues, and retained only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal requirements. Do not send private location histories or backups unless you intentionally want them reviewed. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Policy updates and contact
